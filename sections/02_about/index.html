About

Who's behind the pixels & code.

I’m a hybrid creative developer who bridges design and code, especially for marketing. I work on rich media, motion graphics, email, landing pages, and interactive content. With over 17 years of professional experience, I’ve handled projects ranging from quick-turnaround executions to large-scale campaigns.

Because of this background, I approach projects from both the design and development sides. I’m comfortable leading initiatives or collaborating as part of a team, and I adapt easily to different workflows, timelines, and creative challenges.

My core strengths include creating digital advertisements with interactive elements across multiple platforms using Adobe Creative Suite and HTML5. I build these either fully hardcoded or through web platforms such as DoubleClick Studio, TrueX, Sparkflow, Celtra, GoHighLevel, Litmus, and similar tools, choosing the most appropriate approach based on the speed, scale, and requirements of the campaign.

One of my proudest achievements is the Alexa-Enabled Space Station project for Amazon, which was recognized at the 2019 MediaPost OMMA Awards. The project won in three categories: Artificial Intelligence, Interactive: Single Execution, and UX/UI.
