About

I’m a hybrid creative developer — someone who bridges design and code, especially for marketing, motion graphics, rich media, email, and interactive content. With 14 years of professional experience, I’ve worked on everything from quick-turnaround projects to large-scale campaigns. This range of work has sharpened my skills, enabling me to consistently deliver solutions that support real business objectives.

I’m equally comfortable leading projects or collaborating as part of a team, and I bring a strong mix of creativity, technical skill, and adaptability to every engagement. My core strengths include creating digital advertisements with interactive elements across multiple platforms, using Adobe Creative Suite and HTML5.

One of my proudest achievements is the Alexa-Enabled Space Station project for Amazon, which was recognized at the 2019 MediaPost OMMA Awards. The project won in three categories: Artificial Intelligence, Interactive: Single Execution, and UX/UI.
